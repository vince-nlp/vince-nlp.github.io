ASRS Reports Annotated With Annotator Rationales

This page is a distribution site for the data set composed of ASRS reports annotated with annotator rationales. This data set was introduced in the following paper:

Learning Cause Identifiers from Annotator Rationales.
Muhammad Arshad Ul Abedin, Vincent Ng, and Latifur Rahman Khan.
Proceedings of the 22nd International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI-11), 2011.
