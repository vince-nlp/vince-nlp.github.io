Debate Persuasiveness Dataset


This page is a distribution site of debate data for the task of debate argument persuasiveness scoring. The problem of Debate Argument Persuasiveness Scoring for which this dataset is intended is described in these publications:

  • Isaac Persing and Vincent Ng, Lightly-Supervised Modeling of Argument Persuasiveness. , Proceedings of the 8th International Joint Conference on Natural Language Processing, 2017.
  • Isaac Persing and Vincent Ng, Why Can't You Convince Me? Modeling Weaknesses in Unpersuasive Arguments, Proceedings of the 26th International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2017.


The Debate Argument Persuasiveness Data includes motions, assertions, justifications, error annotations, and persuasiveness scores for debates on 165 topics, as described in the aforementioned publications.
